DIU ERP · Daffodil International University

The problem

Five offices, five copies of the same student

Admission, Registrar, Exams, Accounts and Transport each keep their own records. When one office changes something, the others find out late, or never. The offices depend on each other, but nothing connects them except paper, email and the student.

5offices with separate records
1student, the only link between them
0places where everyone sees the same data
DIU ERP · The problem

What it looks like for a student

Rahim paid on time and still nearly missed his exam

Monday

Pays the semester fee by bKash

Accounts sees the payment in its own ledger. Nobody else does.

Tuesday

Exam office prints admit cards

It works from last week's dues list. Rahim is on the blocked list.

Wednesday

Queues at two offices

Gets a paper receipt from Accounts and carries it across campus to the Exam office.

Thursday

Admit card, 20 minutes before the exam

He sits the exam stressed, after two days of lost class time.

Nothing was wrong with his payment. The update never reached the office that needed it. The same thing happens with dropped courses, section changes, grades and waivers.

DIU ERP · The problem

Our solution

Enter data once, in the office that owns it. Every other office reads it.

One record per student

The applicant's login becomes the student's login. The ID, program and courses live in one place.

Each office owns its data

Accounts writes payments, teachers write marks, Transport writes passes. Others can read but not change them.

Changes flow on their own

A payment clears the exam. A published grade reaches the student. Nobody forwards a list.

One login, many roles

Staff with several jobs switch roles without signing in again. Each screen shows only what that role needs.

Requirement analysis · Functional

What the system must do, by module

Shared core

  • FR-C1One login per person; switch roles without signing out
  • FR-C2Notifications in-app, by email and SMS; each person picks per category
  • FR-C3Help desk routes requests to the right office with a reply deadline

M1 · Admission & Registrar

  • FR-1.1Online application with documents and email verification
  • FR-1.2Admission test, admit card, merit list and online offer
  • FR-1.3Enrolling creates the student ID, login and first invoice

M2 · Student Portal

  • FR-2.1Course registration with live seat counts
  • FR-2.2Section change requests, decided by the department head
  • FR-2.3Results, CGPA, transcripts and certificates online

M3 · Teacher Portal

  • FR-3.1Attendance, with warnings below the threshold
  • FR-3.2Marks entry; the grade sheet locks on submit
  • FR-3.3Department head approves or returns grade sheets

M4 · Accounts

  • FR-4.1Invoices by fee head; the oldest invoice is paid first
  • FR-4.2bKash, Nagad, Rocket, card and cashier-counter payments
  • FR-4.3Waivers, refunds and reversals need a second officer

M5 · Transport

  • FR-5.1Routes, stops, buses and seats per route
  • FR-5.2Bus pass: apply, approve, pay, board with a QR scan
  • FR-5.3Live bus map and delay alerts to riders
